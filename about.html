Who I am

A learning designer and psychology researcher who cares about how people learn, how they feel, and how AI can support both.

Across research labs, an AI startup, and a health-technology company, my approach has stayed the same: understand the people involved, ground the design in evidence, and measure whether it worked.

How I think

Evidence over intuition. Two years in a cognitive science lab, managing data from 100+ participants, taught me to trust what the data says.

Theory as a design tool. For Harvard’s aging project I built an AI dialogue around Erikson’s ego integrity theory, structured as Validation, Meaning, Legacy.

Feedback closes the loop. At Guanxin, input from 60+ learners reshaped course materials and formats. At Vibi, user voices from three communities fed straight back into the product.

What I do

Research

Learning design

AI product

What I’m exploring

How conversational AI can support emotional well-being and learning without replacing human judgment.

I work on both sides of that question: making AI companions feel natural and helpful at Vibi, and mapping the risks generative AI brings to higher education at Tsinghua.
