How I Work

Start with people

Talk to learners and users first. Understand how they think and where they get stuck before building anything.

Ground it in theory

Design from evidence, whether that is Erikson, Gestalt psychology, or the risk literature on generative AI.

Build and measure

Prototype quickly, collect real feedback, and let the data decide the next iteration.
