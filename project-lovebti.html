01The challenge

The team wanted a shareable, engaging entry point into the app that still had a defensible basis in psychology rather than horoscope-style guesswork.

02What I did

  1. 01

    Research the foundations

    Reviewed how established relationship and personality questionnaires are structured and scored.

  2. 02

    Design the questions

    Wrote the question set and mapped answers to result types.

  3. 03

    Write the results

    Drafted result explanations that felt personal and useful rather than generic.

  4. 04

    Build it

    Used Claude to turn the design into an interactive HTML prototype the team could click through and test.

03How it fits together

04Try it yourself

Live prototype. Answer the five questions to get a four-letter Love Type and a share card.

05Outcome

A working prototype the team could click through end to end before committing engineering time. The second version added a “how the type works” screen, richer result analysis, and a share card designed to be posted.
