AI Family Assessment: Parenting-Style Module

Guanxin Lab’s AI assessment gives parents a four-dimension report on their teenager. I designed the module that tells parents about themselves: five parenting-style levels, each with plain-language feedback and a coach’s voice.

01The challenge

The assessment already told parents a lot about their child: family environment, internet use, the parent–child relationship, and school motivation, each with strengths and points to watch. What it did not do was hold up a mirror. Parents finished the report knowing what was wrong and not knowing what to change in themselves. The module had to do that without shaming anyone.

02What I did

  1. 01

    Map the existing report

    Read the four-dimension report end to end and traced where parents got lost: long text, no sense of where they stood, and advice that was not about them.

  2. 02

    Define five levels

    Named and described the ladder, from Level 1 High support to Level 5 High risk / harm, with one-line definitions a parent could recognize themselves in.

  3. 03

    Write the feedback in a coach’s voice

    Drafted “Where you are now” and “What you need” for every level in Teacher Han’s voice, color-coded so the two halves read differently, and paired each level with an icon.

  4. 04

    Lay out the result and hand it over

    Sketched the report section in Modao, annotated the design decisions, and joined product-requirement and technical discussions with the development team.

03How it fits together

04Outcome

A complete content and layout spec the development team could build from, and a report that finally addressed the parent as well as the child. The interactive version above is my English rebuild of that flow.
