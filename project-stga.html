01The challenge

Older adults are often an afterthought in AI product design. The project asked how a conversational agent could support emotional well-being and meaning-making late in life, not just answer questions.

02What I did

  1. 01

    Ground the design in theory

    Drew on Erikson’s concept of ego integrity and Validation Therapy to define what a good conversation should do for this audience.

  2. 02

    Design a three-stage dialogue

    Structured conversations as Validation, then Meaning, then Legacy, moving from emotional acceptance to meaning-making to recording personal memories.

  3. 03

    Test with real users

    Ran sessions with 30+ older adults and synthesized what worked, what confused people, and where the AI missed emotional cues.

  4. 04

    Propose how to measure it

    Wrote a research proposal pairing psychometric scales with linguistic sentiment analysis so outcomes could be evaluated, not assumed.

03How it fits together

04Outcome

Testing insights were synthesized into a research proposal and report that now guide the next design iteration and the evaluation plan.
