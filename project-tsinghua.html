The challenge

Discussion of AI risk in universities was scattered across policy papers and opinion pieces. The project needed a structure that could be evaluated consistently by experts.

What I did

  1. 01

    Review the literature

    Surveyed research on generative AI risks in higher education.

  2. 02

    Build the structure

    Used a Risk Breakdown Structure to organize risks into categories with structured indicators, coded in Excel.

  3. 03

    Design expert validation

    Prepared Delphi survey materials so experts could rate and refine the indicators.

  4. 04

    Document the method

    Collaborated on the methodology report for the risk assessment.

How it fits together

Outcome

A risk framework and survey instrument ready for expert validation, with a documented methodology.
