01The challenge

The product was built in China for a North American audience. Early conversations sounded stiff and generic, and personas drifted between messages.

02What I did

  1. 01

    Test the product

    Used the app as a target user would and logged issues with usability, UI, conversation quality, and missing scenarios, then prioritized them.

  2. 02

    Redesign personas

    Refined character names, personalities, and voice direction, and rewrote English prompts so they read like a real person.

  3. 03

    Structure the coach prompts

    Organized each prompt into four parts: persona, user-input analysis, response strategy, and output constraints on length and wording.

  4. 04

    Support new features

    Reviewed voice-persona fit for coach voice calls and consultation features.

  5. 05

    Bring users back in

    Helped promote across Instagram, Discord, and Threads and designed a free-membership campaign to collect feedback.

03How it fits together

04Outcome

Conversations became more consistent and natural for the target audience, and the four-part prompt structure gave the team a shared template for new personas.
